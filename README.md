# Privacy Policy for Nuriva

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

Nuriva ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Nuriva mobile application.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Nuriva may process limited information necessary to provide the application's functions.

This may include:

- Approximate or precise location information when you choose to use location-based features, such as determining prayer times and Qibla direction.
- Device and application information required for application functionality, security, diagnostics, licensing, and compatibility.
- Information related to application settings and preferences.
- Technical and diagnostic information, including crash and error information.
- Information necessary to provide notifications and other features requested by the user.

Nuriva does not intentionally collect information that is not necessary for providing, securing, maintaining, or improving the application and its services.

## 2. Location Information

Nuriva may request access to your device's location to provide location-dependent features such as:

- Prayer times based on your location.
- Qibla direction.
- Location-related application functions.

Location permission is requested through the operating system. You may deny or revoke this permission through your device settings.

Where possible, location information is used only for the functionality requested by the user.

## 3. Prayer Times and Religious Content

Nuriva provides prayer times, Islamic calendar information, Quran-related features, duas, dhikr, and other religious content.

Some content or calculations may depend on external data sources, calculation methods, downloaded content, or the user's selected location and settings.

Users should independently verify time-sensitive religious information when necessary.

## 4. Quran and Downloadable Content

Nuriva may allow users to access or download Quran pages, translations, tafsir, recitations, or other religious resources.

Downloaded content may be stored locally on the user's device. Removing the application or clearing application data may remove locally stored content.

## 5. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Nuriva may send notifications related to features such as prayer times and reminders.

Notification permissions can be changed at any time through your device settings or, where available, through settings within the application.

## 6. Application Preferences and Local Data

Nuriva may store certain information locally on your device to remember application preferences and provide features such as bookmarks, reading progress, favorites, dhikr counters, Quran or khatm progress, theme selections, and other user preferences.

Local data generally remains on the device unless a feature explicitly requires synchronization or remote processing.

## 7. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Nuriva may use services provided by Google Firebase and other service providers to support application functionality.

These services may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Services used by Nuriva may include functionality related to:

- Application infrastructure.
- Authentication or authorization.
- Database services.
- Crash reporting and diagnostics.
- Security and application integrity.
- Application configuration and version management.

Third-party services are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 8. Licensing and Application Security

Nuriva may process limited device, application, or license-related information for purposes such as:

- Verifying authorized application access.
- Managing licenses.
- Preventing unauthorized use or abuse.
- Maintaining application security.
- Managing application version requirements.

The exact information processed may vary as the licensing and security systems evolve.

## 9. Data Sharing

Nuriva does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve the application.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

## 10. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to technical, security, contractual, and legal requirements.

Data stored locally on the device may remain until the user deletes it, clears application data, or uninstalls the application.

## 11. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed through Nuriva.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Nuriva is not designed to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

If features involving personal information are introduced or materially changed, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of applications and device permissions where appropriate.

## 13. User Choices and Permissions

You can control many permissions granted to Nuriva through your device settings.

Depending on your operating system, these may include:

- Location permission.
- Notification permission.
- Other device permissions required by specific features.

Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from functioning correctly.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nuriva's features, technologies, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
