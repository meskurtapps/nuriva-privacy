Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Nuriva respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the Nuriva mobile application, why that information may be needed, and how it is handled.

1. About the Application

Nuriva is an Islamic mobile application that provides features such as prayer times, Qibla direction, Islamic calendar information, Quran reading, translations and tafsir, recitation features, duas, dhikr, khatm tracking, reminders, notifications, and related worship tools.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you choose to use, Nuriva may process limited information necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve the application.

This may include:

  • Approximate or precise location information when location-based features are enabled
  • Device and application information required for functionality, compatibility, security, and licensing
  • Application settings and user preferences
  • Technical diagnostic information related to errors and crashes
  • Application version and information required for version management
  • Information required to provide notifications and reminders requested by the user

3. Location Information

Nuriva may request access to your device location to provide location-dependent features such as prayer times and Qibla direction.

Location permission is requested through your device's operating system. You may deny or revoke this permission through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent certain location-dependent features from working correctly.

4. Prayer Times and Religious Content

Nuriva provides prayer times, Islamic calendar information, Quran-related features, duas, dhikr, and other religious content.

Some information may depend on the selected location, calculation method, settings, external data sources, or downloaded content. Users should independently verify time-sensitive religious information when necessary.

5. Quran and Downloadable Content

Nuriva may provide access to Quran pages, translations, tafsir, recitations, and other religious resources. Some content may be bundled with the application while other content may be downloaded when requested by the user.

Downloaded content may be stored locally on your device. Removing the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored content.

6. Information Stored on Your Device

Nuriva may store information locally on your device to provide and remember features and preferences.

This may include:

  • Application settings and theme preferences
  • Bookmarks and reading progress
  • Favorites
  • Dhikr counters
  • Quran and khatm progress
  • Downloaded religious content
  • Notification and reminder preferences
  • Other user-selected application preferences

Local information generally remains on your device unless a feature explicitly requires synchronization or remote processing.

7. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Nuriva may send notifications and reminders related to features such as prayer times and other user-selected reminders.

Notification permissions can be changed through your device settings and, where available, within the application.

8. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Nuriva may use Google Firebase and other service providers to support application functionality, security, licensing, database functionality, configuration, diagnostics, crash reporting, and version management.

Depending on the services used, limited technical information about the device and application may be processed by those service providers in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Error and Crash Reports

Nuriva may use Firebase Crashlytics or similar diagnostic services to identify errors, diagnose crashes, and improve application stability.

Diagnostic information may include device type, operating system version, application version, and technical information associated with an error or crash.

10. Licensing and Application Security

Nuriva may process limited device, application, authorization, or license-related information to verify authorized access, manage licenses, prevent abuse, maintain application security, and enforce application version requirements.

11. Permissions

Depending on the features used and the operating system, Nuriva may request permissions such as location and notifications, as well as other permissions required by specific application features.

Permissions can generally be managed through your device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from functioning correctly.

12. Sale and Sharing of Personal Information

Nuriva does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve the application, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

13. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable technical, security, contractual, and legal requirements.

Information stored locally on your device may remain until you delete it, clear application data, or uninstall the application.

14. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed through Nuriva. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Children's Privacy

Nuriva is not designed to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of applications and device permissions where appropriate.

16. Advertising and Future Features

If Nuriva introduces advertising, additional account functionality, premium services, payment-related functionality, new analytics services, or other features that materially change how information is processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Nuriva's features, technologies, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The "Last Updated" date will be revised when this Privacy Policy is updated. The current version will be published on this page.
